Products & Stock

Products appear on the New Bill screen under Products. Stock goes down when you bill and comes back if a bill is cancelled. With a barcode scanner, scan into the search box to add the product.

Staff

Staff are optional. When added, you can pick who did each service, and Reports show each person's work for commission.

Business details (printed on every bill)

GST / Tax

This rate is used for services, and for products set to “same as services”. Since 22 Sep 2025, salon and beauty services are 5% GST (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%) without input tax credit. Each product can have its own rate in the Products tab. Please confirm the rates with your accountant.

Backup & data

The app saves automatically after every change and keeps a daily backup copy for 60 days. To be extra safe, export a backup to a pen drive or Google Drive once a week.
